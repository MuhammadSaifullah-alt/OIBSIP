# Fateh Sultan Mehmed – Tribute Page

A tribute website dedicated to **Fateh Sultan Mehmed (Mehmed the Conqueror)**, the Ottoman Sultan who conquered Constantinople in 1453 and transformed it into the capital of a powerful empire.

The project highlights his life, achievements, legacy, contributions to art and knowledge, and the historic conquest that changed world history.

---

## 📖 About the Project

This tribute page was built using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** as part of a web development internship task.

The website presents important aspects of Sultan Mehmed II's life through a visually rich Ottoman-inspired design featuring:

* Hero Section
* Biography
* Timeline of Achievements
* Conquest of Constantinople
* Famous Quotes
* Patron of Art & Knowledge
* Legacy

---

## ✨ Features

* Responsive design

* Smooth scrolling navigation

## 📚 Sections Included

### 🏛️ Hero Section

Introduction to Fateh Sultan Mehmed with a historical portrait and Ottoman-themed design.

### 📖 Biography

A brief overview of his life, education, rise to power, and achievements.

### ⏳ Timeline

Key events from his birth in 1432 to his passing in 1481.

### ⚔️ Conquest of Constantinople

Highlights the famous 1453 conquest and its significance in history.

### 💬 Famous Quotes

A collection of notable quotes attributed to Mehmed II.

### 🎨 Patron of Art & Knowledge

Showcases his support for education, arts, and architecture.

### 🌍 Legacy

Explains how his achievements influenced the Ottoman Empire and world history.
